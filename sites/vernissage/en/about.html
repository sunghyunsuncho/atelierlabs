WHY VERNISSAGE

How Vernissage
began

It began with a wish to discover beautiful things, learn their stories, and keep them close for longer.

I love going out to see beautiful things: a work that catches my eye in a museum, a landscape I stumble upon while travelling, or a small scene from an ordinary day that stays with me.

There are so many beautiful things in the world that I do not know yet. When I find a work I like, I become curious about the story inside it; learning about its artist leads me to other works and new museums. I love the way one curiosity leads to another discovery.

I wanted a place where artists, artworks, museums, exhibitions and the stories inside them could be explored comfortably together. A place that makes both finding what you know and discovering an unexpected new interest a little easier and more joyful.

Even the works and exhibitions we love fade from memory over time. I wanted somewhere to gather what I have seen and what I want to see, and to take it out again whenever I like.

Vernissage began from these feelings: a place to discover, get to know, and keep the moments that move you, so that when you come back to them, they connect with one another.

You do not need deep knowledge of art. Finding something you like, getting to know it a little more, and remembering a good moment can be enough.

Ready to gather the beautiful things you love?
