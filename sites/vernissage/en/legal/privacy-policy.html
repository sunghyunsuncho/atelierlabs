We have established this policy in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea so that we can protect your personal information and respond promptly to any concerns you may have.

3. Retention and Deletion

  • We delete personal information without delay once the retention period has expired or the purpose for which it was collected has been fulfilled. Electronic files are permanently deleted using methods that prevent recovery, and paper records are shredded or incinerated.
  • Where applicable law requires us to retain certain information, we retain it for the period required by that law.
  • Access and usage records and your notification history are automatically deleted after 180 days, and search history after 90 days.
  • If you delete your account, information that can identify you, such as your email address, name, profile photo and date of birth, is immediately deleted or de-identified, and you will no longer be able to sign in to that account. Your inquiries, push notification tokens, notification settings and notification history are deleted immediately.
  • Activity data you created, such as visit logs, may be retained in de-identified form, with information that identifies you removed, solely for service statistics.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not disclose your personal information to third parties beyond the purposes described in Section 2, except:

  • with your prior consent; or
  • when required by law, such as at the request of an investigative authority in accordance with legal procedures.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access: request information about how we process your personal information
  • Correction and deletion: edit your profile in the app or contact us by email
  • Restriction of processing: ask us to stop processing your personal information
  • Opting out of notifications: turn off each type of notification or withdraw your marketing consent in [My > Notifications] in the app, or turn off notification permission in your device settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at atelierlabs.kr@gmail.com. We will respond without undue delay.

9. Location Information

We provide a location-based service through the "Find nearby museums" feature, and collect and use your location information for this purpose.

  • How we collect it: if you grant location permission, we collect your current coordinates (latitude and longitude) through your device's GPS.
  • How we use it: to find museums near you and show your location on the map.
  • Location information is collected when you use the feature and stored as part of your usage records. We do not share it with third parties.
  • You can withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings. If you do, the "Find nearby museums" feature may not be available.

10. Push Notifications and Marketing Communications

  • If you allow notifications on your device, we send notifications related to your use of the Service, such as reminders about saved exhibitions and events, replies to your inquiries and service announcements. You can change which types you receive at any time in [My > Notifications] in the app.
  • We send marketing communications, such as new docent stories and recommendations, only to users who have separately opted in, as required by the Korean Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection. Marketing notifications are labeled "(Ad)" at the start of the title and include instructions on how to unsubscribe.
  • We do not send marketing notifications between 9:00 p.m. and 9:00 a.m. in your device's time zone.
  • We will notify you of the result whenever you opt in or out, and remind you of your consent status every two years from the date you opted in.
  • You may withdraw your consent to marketing communications at any time in [My > Notifications].

11. Advertising and Advertising Identifiers

To help cover the cost of running the Service, we display ads provided by Google LLC through Google AdMob on certain screens of the mobile app.

  • Information collected: behavioral information such as advertising identifiers (the Android Advertising ID and, only if you allow tracking on iOS, the IDFA), ad impressions and clicks in the app, IP address, and device information
  • How it is collected: Google collects this information automatically through its advertising SDK when an ad is shown in the app. We do not collect or store this information ourselves.
  • Purposes: serving ads, personalizing ads based on your interests, measuring ad performance and preventing invalid clicks
  • Processor and international transfer: Google LLC (United States). Google processes this information in accordance with Google's advertising policies.
  • Retention: behavioral information is retained for the periods set out in Google's policies. Once you reset your advertising identifier, that information is no longer linked to your device.
  • Your choices: you can delete your advertising identifier or turn off personalized ads under [Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking] on iOS, or [Settings > Privacy > Ads] on Android (menu names and locations may vary by device). Opting out does not restrict your use of the Service, but you may still see non-personalized ads.

12. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 14. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 14, we will delete it without delay.
