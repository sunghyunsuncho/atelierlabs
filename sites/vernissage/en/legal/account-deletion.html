Delete Your Vernissage Account and Data

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This English version is provided for convenience only. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the English and Korean versions, the Korean version shall prevail.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

Sign in to the Vernissage app and go to
Profile > Edit > Delete account. Your account will be deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email us at the address below to request account deletion.

To help us verify your request quickly, please include the email address you signed up with (or the details of the social account you used to sign in).

atelierlabs.kr@gmail.com

Data that will be deleted

  • Personally identifiable information, such as your email address, name, date of birth, mobile phone number and profile photo
  • Information linking your account to your social sign-in provider
  • Your inquiries, push notification tokens, notification settings and notification history

Data that may be retained

  • Activity data you created, such as visit logs, may be retained in de-identified form (with information that identifies you removed), solely for service statistics.

Contact: atelierlabs.kr@gmail.com
