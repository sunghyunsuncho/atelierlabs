Art information is scattered everywhere, and few places organize it in depth. Vernissage brings artists, artworks, exhibitions, museums, art movements and the people behind art history together in one place you can search.

On your way out of a gallery, while the work is still vivid, note the date, add a photo and write a few lines. Even if an exhibition or artwork isn't in the search results, you can enter it yourself and log it right away.

Heart the artworks and artists you love and come back to them anytime. Everything you discover and record adds up to an art archive of your own.

Depth,beyond the wiki

Frequently asked questions

What is Vernissage?

Vernissage is an art information hub where you can search artists, artworks, exhibitions, museums and art movements, save what you love and record the art you have seen with photos and notes.

Can I search if I don't remember the exact name?

Yes. Search with whatever you remember. Vernissage combines its own database with open sources such as Wikidata, and supports searching in English, Korean and Japanese.

Which languages does Vernissage support?

The app is available in English, Korean and Japanese. Some content, such as exhibition descriptions written by Korean venues, is shown in its original language.

Can I log exhibitions or artworks that are not in the search results?

Yes. You can enter any museum, exhibition, artwork or artist yourself and add dates, photos and notes.

Which exhibitions are listed?

Current exhibition listings focus on museums and galleries in Korea. Biennales and art fairs are covered worldwide, and the art & culture map shows museums and galleries around the world.

How do I sign in?

Sign in with Apple, Google, Kakao or Naver. Your saved items and journal stay with your account when you change devices.
