Frequently asked questions

What is Vernissage?

Vernissage is an art information hub app. See exhibitions, artists, artworks and museums from around the world in one place, follow connected information as you learn, and record what you experience in your own words. Your entries are gathered on each artist and artwork page, alongside related docent stories.

What information can I find?

Exhibition listings from museums in Korea and abroad, biennials and art fairs around the world, plus artists, artworks, museums, art concepts and art figures. The information keeps growing and updating.

Can I search if I only vaguely remember the name?

Yes. You don't need the exact title or spelling, just type whatever comes to mind. Vernissage searches sources from Korea and around the world in one go, including artist data from Wikidata, the collection of the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea (MMCA) and the Korean Arts Digital Archive.

How do I get started?

Sign in with Apple, Google, Kakao or Naver, then look up an artist or exhibition you're curious about, or log something you saw today. One photo and a single line is all it takes.

Can I log an exhibition or artwork that isn't in the search results?

Yes. You can add museums, exhibitions, artworks and artists yourself and log them right away. Keep the name, date, photos and your thoughts, as much or as little as you like.

How do I find museums and exhibitions nearby?

Browse museums and galleries around you on the art & culture map, or search exhibitions, artists and artworks by name. It's also handy when you're just looking for somewhere to go next.

Where can I find the things I've saved and the entries I've written?

Tap the heart on any artwork, artist, exhibition or museum to keep it in Saved. Everything you write is gathered in the Journal tab in date order, and when you open an artist, exhibition or other detail page, your related entries appear there too.

What are docent stories?

Docent stories are short reads that unpack the context and stories behind artworks and artists, so you can look a little closer. New ones are published all the time, and together with each day's artist and artwork picks, they're a jumping-off point for new discoveries.
