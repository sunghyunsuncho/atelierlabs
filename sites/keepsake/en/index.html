Choose, remember,
and look back together

Dozens of photos pile up every day, yet only a few ever get a second look. Keepsake helps you pick the moments you want to hold on to, write down the story behind them, and come back to them years later.

Pick a few each day, starting right where you left off. Add a short note about how the moment felt, then gather your photos into albums for trips, family, or seasons. Albums point to your originals instead of copying them, so they never take up extra space on your phone.

Photos are better together. Make a shared album with family, friends, or anyone close to you and add the photos each of you kept. Start right away without an account, or sign in to back up your notes, places, and albums for free and pick up on a new phone.

Frequently asked questions

What is Keepsake?

Keepsake is a personal photo book app. Pick the moments you want to keep from all the photos you take, write down the story behind them, and come back to them over time. You can also share albums with the people you love.

Is it free?

Yes. Picking, notes, albums, the Album Map, and cleanup are all free, and signing in lets you back up your notes, places, and albums for free. Only Secure Storage, which keeps the photos and videos themselves, is an optional subscription, and it's coming soon.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start right away without one, and your memories stay on this device. Sign in to back up your notes, places, and albums and pick up on a new phone.

Do more albums take up more space on my phone?

No. Keepsake never copies your photos. Albums simply point to the originals in your photo library, so the same photo can be in many albums without using extra space.

How does Keepsake know where a photo was taken?

It reads the location saved in the photo itself. Keepsake never reads your phone's current location, so no location permission is needed.

How do shared albums work?

The album's creator sends an invite code, and others join by entering it. People without a subscription can view and add photos too. They just need to sign in. Members share the album's storage, each chipping in a little, and creating a shared album requires a Secure Storage subscription.

What is Secure Storage?

An optional subscription that keeps the photos and videos in your secure albums safe, even if you delete them from your phone or lose it. Photos you upload on iPhone show up on Android too (sign in with Google). Space saver keeps photos at a size that still looks sharp on a phone, and you can keep originals for the ones that matter most. It's coming soon.

Does Keepsake delete the photos I didn't keep?

No. Keepsake never deletes photos on its own. When you're done picking, you can look over the photos you didn't keep and choose which ones to clear, only if you want to.
