Delete Your Keepsake Account and Data

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This English version is provided for convenience only. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the English and Korean versions, the Korean version shall prevail.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

Sign in to the Keepsake app and go to
Settings > Account > Delete account. Your account will be deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, send an email to the address below to request account deletion.

To speed up verification, please include the email address of the Apple or Google account you used to sign in.

atelierlabs.kr@gmail.com

Data that will be deleted

  • Account information, such as your account identifier and email address
  • Memories, albums, picking history and photo link information backed up to the server
  • Photos kept in secure albums
  • Shared album membership: you leave every shared album, and the photos you added to shared albums are removed for every member. Album admins are told that the photos were removed.
  • Subscription information and push notification tokens
  • Blocking information (the people you blocked and any blocks of you)
  • Your customer record at RevenueCat

Data that may be retained

  • Shared album report records are kept for 1 year after a report is resolved, to check whether the same user keeps breaking the rules, and are then deleted automatically. Reports made by a deleted account are kept without a link to that account.
  • Under the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, payment event records are kept for 5 years from the date they are received and are then deleted automatically.

Data that stays on your device

  • Memories kept only on your device, and memories linked to photos in your device library, stay on your device after you delete your account. The photos in your device library are not affected.
  • If you used Keepsake without signing in, no information is stored on the server. Memories on your device are removed when you uninstall the app.

Canceling your subscription

  • Deleting your account does not cancel your store subscription. Please cancel it in the subscription settings of the App Store or Google Play.
