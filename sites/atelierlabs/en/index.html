02 / Principles

What we hold to
when we build

01

Choosing has value

You don't need to keep everything. Records gain meaning in the act of choosing what matters.

02

The moment you return

A record shines not when it's saved but when it's revisited. We make past records easy to rediscover.

03

Technology with restraint

Automation and AI help you decide; they don't decide for you. The final choice is always yours.

04

Your records are yours

We ask only for what we need and keep it safe. You should be able to export or delete it whenever you want.

03 / About

Like a small atelier,
one detail at a time.

Atelier Labs is a small studio that makes apps the way an atelier finishes a piece of work. Rather than piling on features, we spend a long time on the one experience that really matters, and aim for apps people can settle into at their own pace.

Some moments slip by easily — an artwork you came across in a museum, a photo you want to see again. We build tools that hold on to those moments so you can come back to them later.
