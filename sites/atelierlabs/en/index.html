02 / About

The beauty of the world,
the moments of your life.

An artwork that stopped you in a museum, an old building you happened upon while traveling, a photo you want to see again. Moments like these make an ordinary day a little richer, yet with time it gets hard to remember even what you loved about them.

Atelier Labs builds tools that hold on to those moments. Vernissage looks outward, to the beauty in the world: it helps you follow your curiosity from an artwork to its artist, to exhibitions and museums, and keep a record of what you've seen. Keepsake looks inward, to your own life: it helps you choose, from countless photos, the moments you truly want to keep, and write down what they mean.

They face different directions, but both start from the same question: what do I love, and what matters to me? Rather than answering it for you, Atelier Labs wants to be there as you find the answer yourself and keep it for a long time — spending time on the one experience that really matters, the way an atelier refines a single piece.

03 / Principles

Principles
behind every app

01

Let curiosity lead

The joy grows when one discovery leads to the next. Rather than taking you straight to a destination, the apps show you the stories along the way.

02

Choosing has value

You don't need to keep everything. When you choose what you loved and add a line of your own, a record truly becomes yours.

03

The moment you return

A record shines not when it's saved but when it's revisited. That's why your records stay together, easy to find again, with room to add to their story over time.

04

Technology for people

Automation and AI help you decide; they don't decide for you. The apps ask only for what they need, and you can export or delete your records anytime.

04 / Vision

Looking
ahead

It starts with art and photos, but the picture is a little bigger than that.

01

Beauty beyond the museum

Ancient artifacts, beautiful architecture, a small flower by the road — they can all move you. The plan is to widen what the apps cover, so anyone can discover and learn about what they love, no expertise required.

02

Records that connect

When artworks and artists, the exhibitions you visited, your notes and the photos you chose stop being scattered and start connecting, they become a small archive of your own taste and experience.

03

From photos to a life's record

Starting from choosing a single photo, the goal is to grow into a personal archive where you choose your own experiences and memories, give them meaning, and look back on your life.

04

Made to last, not to hold you

Not an app that keeps you scrolling, but one you open when you need it and that keeps what matters for a long time. Your records stay safe, and you can always take them with you.

The aim is not more records,
but lives that slowly fill with the things you love.
