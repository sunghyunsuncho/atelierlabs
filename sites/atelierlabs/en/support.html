Frequently asked questions

How do I contact you?

Send an email to atelierlabs.kr@gmail.com. Including the app name, your device (iOS/Android) and the screen where the problem happened helps get it sorted faster.

How do I delete my Vernissage account?

Sign in to the app and go to Profile > Edit > Delete account to delete it right away. If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, send an email with the details of the account you signed up with. See Delete your account and data for more.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.

Please send it. Every email is read and helps shape future updates. Screenshots help a lot.
