FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I contact you?

Email us at atelierlabs.kr@gmail.com. Including the app name, your device (iOS/Android) and the screen where the problem happened helps us help you faster.

How do I delete my Vernissage account?

Sign in to the app and go to Profile > Edit > Delete account to delete it right away. If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email us with the details of the account you signed up with. See Delete your account and data for more.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.

We'd love to hear it. We read every email and use it to shape future updates. Screenshots help a lot.
